1 · Sign in

Sign-in is passkey only: no email and no password. One button triggers Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello. There is no sign-up page. The first passkey is enrolled once through /setup?token=… (right), and that page then locks itself.

4 · Selecting text → highlight or note

Selecting text brings up a floating toolbar above the selection. A color click highlights it, and Note creates a note anchored to the selection and focuses its editor in the right pane. Clicking an existing highlight brings the toolbar back with "Remove" and a different color.

7 · Mobile (< 768px)

There is no room for a split, so the Bible takes the full width. A ✎ in the gutter marks verses with notes. Tapping it opens a bottom sheet with those notes, and dragging the sheet up shows every note for the chapter. Swipe left or right to change chapters in page mode.
